To convert watt hours to mAh, multiply the watt hours by 1,000 and divide by the battery voltage. Most phone and power bank cells are 3.7 volts, so a 37 Wh power bank holds 10,000 mAh. To go the other way, from mAh to watt hours, multiply the mAh by the voltage and divide by 1,000; the calculator above works in both directions.

The formulas

  • mAh = Wh × 1,000 ÷ volts
  • Wh = mAh × volts ÷ 1,000

Milliamp hours on their own do not tell you how much energy a battery holds, because the same mAh at a higher voltage stores more energy. Watt hours include the voltage, which is why airlines, laptop makers and solar equipment use them. If you compare two batteries with different voltages, convert both to watt hours first.

Why a 10,000 mAh power bank does not charge a phone four times

Power banks advertise the capacity of their internal cells at 3.7 volts. To charge a phone, the bank raises the voltage to 5 volts or more for the USB connection, and the phone then converts it back down to charge its own battery. Each step loses some energy as heat. In practice a power bank delivers roughly two thirds of its rated capacity to the phone, so a 10,000 mAh bank gives about 6,000 to 7,000 mAh of phone battery.

Power banks on planes

Airlines limit spare lithium batteries by watt hours. Under the rules most airlines follow, batteries up to 100 Wh may go in your carry on bag, batteries from 100 to 160 Wh need the airline's approval, and anything larger is not allowed. Spare batteries and power banks must not go in checked luggage. At 3.7 volts, 100 Wh is about 27,000 mAh, so a 20,000 mAh power bank (74 Wh) is normally fine. Always check your airline's current rules before you fly.

Worked examples

  • 20,000 mAh power bank: 20,000 × 3.7 ÷ 1,000 = 74 Wh.
  • 99 Wh laptop battery at 11.55 V: 99 × 1,000 ÷ 11.55 = about 8,570 mAh.
  • 5,000 mAh phone battery at 3.85 V: 5,000 × 3.85 ÷ 1,000 = 19.25 Wh.

Frequently asked questions

How many mAh is 100 Wh?

At 3.7 volts, 100 Wh is about 27,027 mAh. At 5 volts it is 20,000 mAh, and at 12 volts it is about 8,333 mAh.

How do I convert mAh to watt hours?

Multiply the mAh by the battery voltage and divide by 1,000. A 10,000 mAh bank at 3.7 volts is 37 Wh.

What voltage should I use for a power bank?

Use 3.7 volts, the nominal voltage of the lithium cells inside, unless the label states a different cell voltage. Do not use the 5 volt USB output voltage, which gives a lower and misleading mAh figure.

Is a higher mAh always better?

Only when the voltage is the same. A 3,000 mAh battery at 12 volts holds far more energy than a 5,000 mAh battery at 3.7 volts. Compare watt hours to be sure.
