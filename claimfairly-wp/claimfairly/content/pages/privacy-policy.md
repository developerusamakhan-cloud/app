Short version: the numbers and details you type into our calculators never leave your device. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use any tool.

*Last updated: {{today}}*

## What you type into calculators

All calculators, including the demand letter generator, run entirely in your web browser. Nothing you enter is sent to our server, saved in a database or shared with anyone. When you close the page, it is gone. PDFs are created on your own device.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, added through Google's Site Kit plugin, to see which pages and tools people find useful. It uses first-party cookies and records things like the pages you visit, how you arrived, your approximate location (country or city), and your device and browser type. We do not send your name, email or anything you type into Google Analytics.

When you use a calculator, we also record a simple usage event, such as "the diminished value calculator showed a result" or "a demand letter was downloaded as a PDF." These events contain only the tool name and the action. They never include the amounts, dates, names or other details you entered.

You can learn how Google uses this data in [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites), and you can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Server logs

Like almost every website, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and to keep the site running. These logs are not used to identify you.

## Email

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply. We do not add you to any list.

## Advertising

If we show ads through Google AdSense or a similar network, those partners may use cookies to show and measure ads, including ads based on your interests. You can control personalized ads in [Google's Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) and learn more at [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). We will update this page before any ads are turned on.

## Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

You can use every tool without giving us any personal information. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

## Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Questions: [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}).

## Data security

Because the calculators do not send or store what you type, there is no calculator data on our servers to protect or leak. The site is served over HTTPS. If we ever add a feature that stores information, such as a newsletter, this policy will be updated first to explain what is collected and why.
