People use ClaimFairly when money and their health are on the line. That is a reason to be careful, so here is exactly how pages on this site are made and kept up to date.

## Who writes and reviews

Pages are written and checked by {{founder}}, who runs the site. We are not a law firm and do not give legal advice. When a topic needs a professional opinion, we say so on the page.

## How we choose sources

We rank sources in this order:

1. **Official sources:** state statutes, court decisions, state insurance departments and federal agencies.
2. **Established legal references:** for example the Legal Information Institute at Cornell Law School.
3. **Recognized industry and consumer sources**, when official sources do not cover a topic.

We do not use anonymous forums, lead-generation sites or AI-generated summaries as sources. Every page lists its sources at the bottom.

## How formulas are chosen

Our calculators use methods that are already used in real claims as a starting point, like the 17c diminished value formula or the multiplier method. We explain every formula, show every assumption, and point out when a method tends to favor the insurer.

## How often pages are reviewed

- **State pages** are reviewed at least every three months, and sooner when we learn about a law change.
- **Calculators and guides** are reviewed at least every six months.
- Every page shows a **"Last reviewed"** date. We only change that date when someone has actually checked the page against its sources.

## Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it quickly and update the reviewed date. For significant errors, like a wrong filing deadline, we add a short note to the page explaining what changed.

Spotted a problem? Email [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}). Please include the page and, if you can, a link to the source that shows the correct information.

## Independence

No insurer, law firm or advertiser can pay to change a formula, a result or an article. Ads, if shown, are kept outside the calculators.
