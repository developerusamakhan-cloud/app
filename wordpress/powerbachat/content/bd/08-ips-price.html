What an IPS does

An IPS (instant power supply) is an inverter with a built-in charger. When grid power is available, it charges the battery. When the power goes off, it switches over within a fraction of a second and runs your fans and lights from the battery. Most home IPS units are connected to a few selected circuits, not the whole house, so only the most important loads run during an outage.

Choosing the right VA size

IPS units are rated in VA (volt-amperes). As a rough guide, the IPS should handle the total watts you want to run at once, with some headroom for fans and motors that draw extra when starting:

  • Around 600 to 700 VA: two or three fans and several LED lights.
  • Around 1,000 to 1,100 VA: four or five fans, lights, a TV and a router.
  • Larger units: bigger homes or a small fridge on backup.

The VA rating decides how much you can run at once. The battery decides how long it runs. Our IPS calculator shows both for your own appliances.

Sine wave or square wave

Sine wave IPS units produce power similar to the grid. Fans run quietly, and sensitive electronics, fridges and LED drivers work as they should. Square wave or modified sine wave units are cheaper but can make fans hum and may not suit some appliances. For most homes, a sine wave IPS is worth the extra cost.

Popular brands

Rahimafrooz, through its Instapower range, has long been a familiar name in Bangladesh. Luminous and Microtek, both well known in South Asia, are widely available, as are local brands such as Vision. All offer units from small to large VA ratings. When comparing, look at the waveform, warranty, availability of service centres near you, and whether the unit supports the battery type you want.

Total cost: IPS plus battery

The full IPS price in Bangladesh for a working setup is the unit plus one or two batteries, plus wiring. For a typical home, the battery often costs as much as or more than the IPS itself. Planning the battery first, based on how many hours of backup you need, usually leads to a better and cheaper choice.

Installation tips

  • Have a qualified electrician connect the IPS to selected circuits, usually fans and lights.
  • Keep the IPS and battery in a ventilated place, off the floor and away from water.
  • Use correctly sized cables between the battery and the IPS.
  • Keep high-power appliances, such as irons, heaters and kettles, off the IPS circuit.

Before you buy: a short checklist

  1. List the fans and lights you want on backup and add up their watts.
  2. Decide how many hours of backup you need from your area's usual cuts.
  3. Use the IPS calculator to choose the VA rating and battery size.
  4. Choose a sine wave unit with a service centre near you.
  5. Ask for recent battery stock, a proper invoice and the warranty card.
  6. Have a qualified electrician wire the IPS to the right circuits.

Common mistakes

  • Buying a large IPS with a small battery. It can run a lot at once, but only for minutes.
  • Putting the whole house on the IPS. Heavy loads drain the battery and can damage the unit.
  • Ignoring ventilation. A battery in a closed cupboard runs hot and ages fast.
  • Never topping up water. Tubular batteries fail early when the plates dry out.

Avoiding these mistakes matters more than a small difference in the IPS price in Bangladesh between two shops.

IPS and your electricity bill

An IPS recharges its battery from the grid after every cut, and some energy is lost in the process. Older batteries and less efficient units waste more. A typical home IPS can add 20 to 50 units a month. Use the electricity bill calculator to see what that costs. Efficient fans and LED lights on the IPS circuit reduce both the backup load and the recharge units.

IPS or solar?

If you face long or frequent load-shedding, a solar system with batteries can charge during the day without using grid units, and can lower your bill too. It costs more upfront. Our guide to IPS or solar in Bangladesh compares the two for different homes.

Warranty and service

An IPS is a long-term purchase, and service matters as much as price. Before buying, find out where the nearest authorised service centre is, how long repairs usually take, and whether the warranty covers parts and labour. Keep the invoice and warranty card in a safe place. Units from established brands with service networks across Bangladesh are easier to repair years later, when a cheap unknown unit may have no parts available.

Upgrading an existing IPS

If your current IPS works but backup is too short, you may not need a new unit. A healthier or larger battery, if the IPS supports it, can restore backup time. If the IPS keeps showing overload, you need a larger unit or fewer loads on the circuit.

A little planning before you buy almost always costs less than replacing the wrong unit later.

Frequently asked questions

What is the IPS price in Bangladesh?

See the price table above for current prices of popular IPS units. Batteries are extra.

What size IPS do I need for three fans and lights?

Usually around 600 to 700 VA. Use the IPS calculator with your exact appliances to check.

Which battery should I use with my IPS?

A tubular battery designed for IPS use is the standard choice. Lithium works with IPS units that support it and lasts longer.

Can an IPS run a fridge?

A larger sine wave IPS can run a small fridge, but the fridge's starting surge needs extra capacity.

How long does an IPS unit last?

A good IPS unit often lasts many years. The battery usually needs replacing first.

Is a sine wave IPS worth the extra cost?

For most homes, yes. Fans run quieter and electronics and fridges work as they should.
