One ton of air conditioning equals 12,000 BTU per hour, so to convert BTU to tons you divide by 12,000. A 24,000 BTU unit is 2 tons, and a 36,000 BTU central system is 3 tons. To go from tons to BTU, multiply by 12,000; the calculator above works in both directions.

Why a ton is 12,000 BTU

The unit comes from the days of cooling with ice. Melting one ton (2,000 pounds) of ice absorbs about 288,000 BTU of heat. Spread over 24 hours, that is 12,000 BTU per hour. A "1 ton" air conditioner removes heat at the same rate as a ton of ice melting in a day, about 3.52 kW of cooling.

  • tons = BTU/h ÷ 12,000
  • BTU/h = tons × 12,000

What size air conditioner do you need?

A common rule of thumb is about 20 BTU per hour for each square foot of living space, so a 500 square foot room needs around 10,000 BTU and a 1,500 square foot home around 30,000 BTU, or 2.5 tons. Add capacity for sunny rooms, kitchens, top floors, poor insulation and very hot climates, and less for shaded, well insulated rooms.

Rules of thumb are only a starting point. An oversized unit cools quickly but switches on and off often and leaves the air humid; an undersized one runs constantly on the hottest days. For central systems, ask an installer for a proper load calculation, such as Manual J in the United States.

In South Asia split air conditioners are usually sold by tonnage: a 1 ton unit is 12,000 BTU, 1.5 ton is 18,000 BTU and 2 ton is 24,000 BTU. To see what running one costs, use the BTU to watts calculator together with your local bill calculator.

Worked examples

  • 18,000 BTU: 18,000 ÷ 12,000 = 1.5 tons.
  • 42,000 BTU: 42,000 ÷ 12,000 = 3.5 tons.
  • 5 ton system: 5 × 12,000 = 60,000 BTU per hour.

Frequently asked questions

How many BTU are in a ton?

One ton of refrigeration is 12,000 BTU per hour.

How many tons is 24,000 BTU?

24,000 BTU per hour is 2 tons.

Is an AC ton the same as a ton of weight?

No. A ton of cooling describes how fast the unit removes heat, not how heavy it is. A 1 ton split AC weighs far less than a ton.

How many kW is a 1.5 ton AC?

A 1.5 ton air conditioner provides about 5.28 kW of cooling. It typically uses around 1.3 to 1.8 kW of electricity at full output, depending on its efficiency.
