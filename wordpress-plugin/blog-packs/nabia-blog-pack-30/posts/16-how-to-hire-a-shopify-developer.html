Shopify is built to be easy, so many people who set up one store call themselves Shopify developers. That is fine for simple jobs. For a store that is your business, a few questions separate the experts from the enthusiasts.

What a Shopify developer does

From store setup and theme customisation to custom themes coded in Liquid, product configurators, metafields, checkout extensions and integrations. I explain the levels in Shopify development explained.

Decide what you need first

  • Setup and design tweaks: a good Shopify designer or site builder can handle it.
  • Custom sections, features or speed work: you need a developer comfortable with Liquid and JavaScript.
  • Integrations with other systems: look for API experience.

What to check

  • Live stores, tested on your phone, including checkout
  • Reviews you can verify, for example on Upwork, Fiverr or the Shopify Partner directory
  • Clear communication in the first conversation
  • Questions about your products, customers and goals

Red flags

  • Recommending lots of paid apps for simple needs
  • No mention of store speed
  • Wanting the store created under their account
  • Vague quotes like "starting from" without a scope

What it costs

Store setups commonly cost $1,000 to $5,000 depending on products, design and features. Ongoing costs include your Shopify plan, apps and payment fees. I break this down in how much does a Shopify store cost.

How to brief a Shopify developer

  • What you sell, how many products and variants
  • Where you ship and how you price shipping
  • Stores you like, and what specifically you like
  • Apps you already use and why
  • Budget, deadline and who will run the store day to day

Freelancer, agency or Shopify Expert?

For most small brands, an experienced freelancer offers the best mix of quality, cost and communication. Agencies suit large brands needing full teams. Whatever you choose, make sure the store and theme are owned by you.
