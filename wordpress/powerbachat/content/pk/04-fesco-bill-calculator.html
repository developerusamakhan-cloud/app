Put your units into the tool above and you get the slab charges, GST, electricity duty and an estimated total, before the paper bill arrives.

Faisalabad is a city of busy households. Many homes run a small workshop, a stitching machine or a home business alongside the family's fans and ACs, and that shows up in the units. Knowing in advance what a reading will cost helps you plan, especially in the hot months when a single week of heavy use can tip you into a higher slab.

How to get a reliable estimate

A good estimate starts with a good reading. Here is the routine we suggest:

  1. Keep last month's bill handy. Note the present reading in the meter box.
  2. Read your meter on the same day each week. Write it on the wall near the meter or in your phone.
  3. Subtract to get units used. Divide by days and multiply by 30 for the full month.
  4. Enter the figure in the calculator. Switch on "Protected consumer" only if your last six bills were all at 200 units or less.

A weekly reading takes thirty seconds and tells you much more than a monthly surprise.

A worked example from Faisalabad

Here is a typical example of how the calculator helps. A family in Peoples Colony reads the meter every Sunday. Their readings for the first three weeks of June show 95, 110 and 120 units, a total of 325 units with a week still left. The trend says they will finish around 450 units.

Entering 450 in the FESCO bill calculator shows their estimated bill and, in the breakdown, that the last 50 units sit in the 401 to 500 slab. If they hold the final week to about 75 units instead of 125, the bill stays inside the 301 to 400 slab and the top slab disappears. Two fewer AC hours a night and a cooler in the lounge during the afternoon are enough. This is exactly the sort of decision the weekly reading makes possible, and it costs nothing but a few seconds each Sunday.

Summer and winter in the FESCO region

Central Punjab has a sharp seasonal pattern. Between November and February most homes sit well under 200 units, because fans are off and ACs are idle. From April the units climb steadily, and June and July are almost always the peak. If you are protected in winter, the first hot month is when you are most at risk of losing that status. If you are already non-protected, the winter months are when the per unit average falls, because most of your units land in the cheaper first slabs.

When the meter reading is estimated

Sometimes a bill shows an estimated or average reading, usually marked on the bill, because the meter reader could not access the meter. The next bill then catches up, and the catch-up units can push you into a higher slab for that month. If you see an estimated reading, photograph your meter with the date visible and take it to your subdivision. Getting the reading corrected quickly is usually easier than disputing a large catch-up bill later.

Home workshops and FESCO tariffs

Running a power loom, a sewing unit or a small workshop from home is common across Faisalabad. Strictly, commercial or industrial work should be on a commercial or industrial connection, which uses different tariffs from the household slabs in this calculator. If your home meter carries a business load, your bill will rise quickly through the slabs. In some cases a separate connection for the business works out cheaper, and it also keeps your household history clean for protected status. Ask at your FESCO subdivision before deciding, because the rules and fees vary by load.

What else appears on a FESCO bill

  • FPA: the fuel price adjustment, charged or credited per unit and changed every month. Read the FPA guide.
  • GST and electricity duty: included in this calculator's estimate.
  • Arrears: any amount left from earlier bills.
  • Surcharge: added after the due date.

Saving units in a Faisalabad home

  • Move heavy work to daytime. If you run machines, daytime hours are when solar can help most later on, and the home load is lighter.
  • Switch old motors for efficient ones. Old water pumps and sewing motors waste a surprising amount.
  • Keep the AC filter clean. Textile lint and dust in Faisalabad clog filters quickly.
  • Size solar for daytime load. A home with daytime machines gets more value from solar than an evening-only home. Our 5kW solar system price page shows what a typical setup costs.

Frequently asked questions

Is the FESCO bill calculator updated for this year?

Yes. We check the slab rates against the latest notification every month. The last check was [powerbachat_checked], and the date is also shown under the calculator.

Why does my FESCO bill not match the calculator?

The calculator estimates the base bill. Your printed bill also includes the monthly fuel adjustment and may include arrears or a surcharge. If the units are wrong, raise it with your subdivision before the due date.

Can I use this for a FESCO commercial connection?

No. Commercial and industrial connections use different tariffs. This FESCO bill calculator is for household connections only.

Does FESCO charge different rates in Sargodha or Jhang?

No. All FESCO districts use the same national tariff, and so does every other company, including GEPCO and MEPCO.
