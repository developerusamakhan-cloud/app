Bill estimates

Our bill calculators apply the published slab rates, fixed charges, adjustments and taxes to the units you enter. Your official bill may differ because of:

  • Arrears, instalments or late payment charges from previous months.
  • Meter rent, service charges or local taxes that apply to some connections only.
  • Monthly or quarterly adjustments announced after our last update.
  • A different tariff category, sanctioned load or connection type from the one assumed.
  • Estimated or corrected meter readings, or a billing period of a different length.
  • State or government subsidies that depend on eligibility we cannot check.

If our estimate and your bill disagree, your official bill is the final word. Our calculators show each step so you can see where the difference arises, and you can raise it with your electricity company.

Rates and dates

Tariffs change, sometimes at short notice. We show the date we last checked the rates at the top of every page and update the tables when new notifications are issued. There can be a gap between a new rate taking effect and our update. For decisions that depend on a precise rate, check the latest notification from the regulator or your electricity company.

Solar, battery and IPS figures

System sizes, generation, savings, payback periods, subsidy amounts and equipment prices on the site are estimates for planning and comparison. Real results depend on your roof direction and shade, local weather, equipment quality, installation, your usage pattern and the policies of your electricity company, including net metering or net billing rules that can change. Prices vary between cities, sellers and seasons. Always get written quotes from qualified installers, check warranties, and confirm subsidy eligibility with the official scheme before you buy.
