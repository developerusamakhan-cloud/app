Progressive is one of the largest auto insurers in the United States, sold both directly and through independent agents, and known for programs like Snapshot and Name Your Price. Here is how a car accident claim with Progressive typically works, whether you are the policyholder or the other driver.

[cf_note type="info"]ClaimFairly is independent and not affiliated with Progressive. This page is general information based on publicly available sources. For your policy's exact terms and the current process, check Progressive's [official claims page](https://www.progressive.com/claims/) or your policy documents.[/cf_note]

## How to file a claim with Progressive

Most large insurers, Progressive included, let you start a claim in several ways:

- **Online** through the claims section of their website
- **In their mobile app**
- **By phone** with a claims representative
- **Through an agent**, if you bought your policy through one

Progressive customers can usually start a claim online, in the app, by phone or through an independent agent if they bought the policy that way. Progressive has also operated service centers in some areas where an adjuster can inspect the car and coordinate repairs.

### What to have ready

| Item | Why it helps |
|---|---|
| Date, time and location of the crash | Basic claim details |
| The other driver's name, plate and insurer | Needed to identify the other party |
| Police report number | Supports the fault decision |
| Photos of both vehicles and the scene | Speeds up the damage estimate |
| Witness names and phone numbers | Helps if fault is disputed |
| A short description of what happened | Keeps your account consistent |

**If you are the other driver** (not a Progressive customer), you can usually open a third-party claim the same way. Ask for the claim number and the name of the adjuster assigned to you, and write both down.

## What happens after you file

1. **An adjuster is assigned.** They review the report, photos and statements and decide on fault.
2. **Damage is assessed** through photos in the app, an inspection or an estimate from a repair shop.
3. **Property damage is paid** for repairs, or for the car's value if it is a total loss.
4. **Rental coverage** may apply, either under your own policy or as part of a third-party claim when their driver is at fault.
5. **Injury claims are handled separately** and usually take longer, because the adjuster waits for medical records and bills.

## A typical timeline

| Stage | Usual time |
|---|---|
| Claim reported and adjuster assigned | Days |
| Fault decision on a clear crash | Days to a few weeks |
| Repair estimate and approval | One to two weeks |
| Repairs completed | Depends on the shop and parts |
| Injury claim offer | Usually after treatment ends |

## Repairs

Progressive works with a network of repair shops and has offered a guarantee on repairs done at network shops for as long as you own the car. You can usually choose your own shop instead. Ask how estimates, supplements for hidden damage, and payment will be handled if you go outside the network.

If your car was repaired after a crash that was not your fault, you may also be able to claim **diminished value**, the resale value the car lost because of its accident history. ## Total loss claims

If repairs would cost too much compared with the car's value, the car may be declared a **total loss**. The insurer pays its actual cash value before the crash, minus your deductible if you claim under your own policy. Check their valuation against listings for similar cars in your area. If you owe more than the payout on a loan or lease, gap insurance may cover the difference.

## Injury claims

For injuries, the adjuster will ask for medical records, bills and proof of lost wages. The offer is usually built from your economic losses plus an amount for pain and suffering, reduced for any fault they assign to you. ## Using your own coverage

Even when the other driver was at fault, your own policy may help:

- **Collision coverage** pays to fix your car quickly, minus your deductible, and your insurer may recover the cost later.
- **MedPay or PIP** pays medical bills regardless of fault, up to your limit.
- **Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage** helps when the other driver has no insurance or too little.
- **Rental reimbursement** covers a rental car if you bought it.

## Tips for dealing with Progressive

- **Get everything in writing:** the claim number, the adjuster's name and any offer.
- **Be careful with recorded statements** if you are claiming against their policyholder. You can politely decline or answer in writing.
- **Do not sign a release** until you know the full cost of your injuries.
- **Keep copies** of every document and photo you send.
- **Put your demand in writing** with our [demand letter generator](/demand-letter-generator/).
- If you are not a Progressive customer and are claiming against their policyholder, you will deal with the liability side of the claim. Get the claim number early and keep communication in writing when you can.

## If you disagree with a decision

Ask the adjuster to explain the decision in writing and point to the policy language or evidence they relied on. Send any missing documents. If you still cannot resolve it, ask to speak with a supervisor, file a complaint with your state's department of insurance, or talk to a licensed attorney.
