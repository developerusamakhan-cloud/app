A concussion is a mild traumatic brain injury. You do not have to hit your head to get one. A hard jolt can be enough. Symptoms like headaches, dizziness, trouble concentrating, sensitivity to light and sleep changes can show up hours or days after the crash.

Because concussions rarely show on a CT scan, they are easy for an insurer to downplay. Careful documentation makes the difference.

## Estimate your concussion claim

The calculator is preset to **moderate**. Many concussions resolve in days to weeks, which fits minor. If symptoms lasted months or affected your work, moderate or serious may fit better.

## Why concussion claims are hard to value

- **They are often invisible on imaging.** Diagnosis relies on symptoms and clinical exams.
- **Symptoms vary a lot.** Most people recover within days to a few weeks. Some develop lasting symptoms, sometimes called post-concussion syndrome.
- **The effects are often cognitive.** Trouble focusing at work or remembering things is real, but it does not produce big medical bills. That can make the multiplier method undervalue the claim. The [per diem method](/pain-and-suffering-calculator/) can help show the impact of a long recovery.

## Documentation that matters

- **Early medical evaluation**, ideally the same day or next day
- **A symptom diary**: headaches, dizziness, sleep, concentration, mood
- **Follow up with a specialist**, such as a neurologist, if symptoms continue
- **Work records** showing reduced hours, accommodations or missed deadlines
- **Statements** from family or coworkers about changes they noticed

## Warning signs to take seriously

Get emergency care right away for a worsening headache, repeated vomiting, slurred speech, confusion, unusual drowsiness, weakness or a seizure. Your health comes first. The claim can wait.

## What can change your number

- Length of symptoms and whether they are ongoing
- Specialist diagnosis and testing
- Impact on your job or schooling
- Your state's no-fault threshold, if you live in a no-fault state
- The at-fault driver's policy limits
