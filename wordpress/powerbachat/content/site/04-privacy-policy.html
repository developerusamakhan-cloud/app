This privacy policy explains what information PowerBachat collects when you use the site, why we collect it, how long we keep it and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain. The guiding rule is that we collect as little as possible: you can use every calculator on the site without giving us any personal information at all.

Last updated: [powerbachat_updated]

Who we are

PowerBachat ("we", "us") runs the website you are reading, which offers electricity bill calculators, unit rate information, solar tools and guides for Pakistan, India and Bangladesh. If you have a question about this policy, you can reach us through our contact page or at [powerbachat_contact_email].

Calculators: nothing leaves your browser

When you type your units, choose an electricity company or set the size of a solar system, the calculation runs in your browser. We do not send those numbers to our servers and we do not store them. Closing the page clears them.

Your country

To show the right country, the site needs to know roughly where you are. We do this in the least intrusive way we can:

  • If our hosting or content delivery network tells us your country, we use that.
  • Otherwise, our server may ask a country lookup service which country your IP address belongs to. The service returns only a country code. We keep the answer in a short-term cache for up to a week so we do not need to ask again; the cache is keyed to your IP address and deleted automatically when it expires.
  • We then remember the country in a small cookie called pb_cc for 30 days, so the site does not need to look it up on every visit.

We never try to find your city, street or precise location, and we do not build a profile of you. The cookie contains only a two-letter country code.

Tariff alerts

If you join our tariff alerts, we collect your email address and the country you were viewing, plus the date you signed up and confirmed. We use them only to send short messages about tariff changes, adjustments, price moves and new guides for your country. We send a confirmation email first, and nothing else is sent until you confirm. Every message has an unsubscribe link that works with one click. When you unsubscribe, we stop sending immediately and delete your address within 30 days, keeping only what we need to make sure you are not emailed again.

If the site uses an outside email service to send alerts, your address is stored with that service under its own privacy terms, and only for this purpose.

Contact form

When you send a message through our contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. We use them to reply and, where relevant, to correct the site. We keep messages for up to 12 months and then delete them, unless the conversation is ongoing. Please do not send personal details from your bill, such as your account number or address; we never need them.

Server logs and security

Like almost every website, our hosting keeps short-term technical logs, which can include your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. These logs are used to keep the site running and secure, for example to block abusive traffic, and are deleted on a rolling basis by our hosting provider.

Fonts and outside services

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser downloads a font, Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. No cookies are set by this. If the site later adds analytics, advertising or video embeds, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first. See our cookie policy for the full list of cookies.

Why we are allowed to use your information

Where data protection law applies, we rely on:

  • Consent for tariff alerts, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Legitimate interests for showing the right country, replying to messages and keeping the site secure. These uses are limited and expected, and they do not override your rights.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with electricity companies, solar installers or advertisers. We only share it with service providers that help us run the site, such as our hosting provider and, if used, an email service, and only as far as they need to provide that service. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Country lookups: up to 7 days in cache.
  • The pb_cc cookie: 30 days in your browser.
  • Tariff alert subscriptions: until you unsubscribe, then deleted within 30 days.
  • Unconfirmed sign-ups: deleted after 30 days.
  • Contact messages: up to 12 months.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can unsubscribe from alerts at any time. You can clear or block cookies in your browser; the site will still work, although it may need to detect your country again. To make a request, use our contact page. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you before acting on it.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can try to fix it. You may also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

PowerBachat is meant for adults who manage household bills. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has signed up for alerts, contact us and we will delete the address.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection, access to our systems is limited, and we keep software up to date. No system is perfectly secure, but we collect so little that the risk to you is small.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell alert subscribers by email. Please also read our terms of use.
