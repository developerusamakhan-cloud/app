Quick answer: a church website should include service times and location, a Plan your visit page, kids and youth information, beliefs, staff, sermons, events and groups, online giving and an easy way to contact you. Behind the scenes it also needs mobile-friendly design, a privacy policy, fast loading and simple editing for volunteers.

Use this checklist with your team to review your current site, or as a brief for a new one. Tick off what you have; the gaps are your to-do list.

Homepage

  • Service times and address in the first screen
  • A "Plan your visit" button
  • A real photo of your church family
  • A one-sentence welcome that says who you are
  • Links to sermons, events and giving

Plan your visit

  • What to expect: length, style of worship, what to wear
  • Parking and entrance, with a photo
  • Accessibility information
  • What happens for children on arrival
  • An optional form to let you know they are coming

About and beliefs

  • Your story in a few paragraphs
  • What you believe in plain language, with a link to a full statement
  • Denomination or network, if any
  • Staff and leaders with photos and short bios

Kids and youth

  • Age groups and what they do
  • Safety and check-in process
  • Leaders' names and photos

Sermons and media

  • Recent sermons, newest first
  • Organised by series, speaker and topic
  • Video or audio, plus notes if you have them

Events, groups and serving

  • Upcoming events only, with registration where needed
  • Small groups with day, time, place and leader
  • Serving opportunities with a sign-up form

Giving

  • A secure online giving platform
  • One clear "Give" button in the menu and footer
  • How gifts are used, and other ways to give

Contact

  • Short contact form, phone, email and office hours
  • A prayer request form, if you offer it
  • Links to social media and livestream

Behind the scenes

  • Works well on phones; tap to call and tap for directions
  • Fast loading, compressed images
  • Privacy policy and cookie notice if you use analytics
  • Google Business Profile with matching details
  • Simple editing and a short guide for volunteers
  • Regular updates and backups (see website maintenance)
