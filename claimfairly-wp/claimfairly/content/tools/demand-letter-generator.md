## What goes into a strong demand letter

1. **The basics up front.** Your name, the claim number, the insurer's policyholder and the date of the crash. It saves the adjuster time and keeps your letter from getting lost.
2. **Facts, not feelings.** Where you were, what the other driver did, what the police report says. Keep it short and specific.
3. **Your injuries and treatment**, in the order they happened: ER visit, diagnosis, therapy, follow ups.
4. **An itemized list of damages.** Every bill, with the provider and amount. Adjusters check the math, so make it easy.
5. **A clear number.** Name the amount you are asking for. It is normal to open higher than you expect to settle.
6. **A deadline.** Thirty days is common. It shows you are organized without being aggressive.

## How much should you ask for?

Most people use the high end of a realistic range as their opening demand. Our [car accident settlement calculator](/car-accident-settlement-calculator/) gives you that range, and its "Write a demand letter" button carries the numbers straight into this form.

Avoid asking for more than the other driver's policy limit unless you have a plan for the difference, like an underinsured motorist claim with your own insurer. Asking for an amount the policy cannot pay rarely speeds things up.

## Mistakes that weaken a demand letter

- **Admitting fault** or apologizing for the crash. Stick to what happened.
- **Guessing about injuries** you have not been diagnosed with.
- **Leaving out small costs.** Parking at the hospital, prescriptions and mileage to appointments add up.
- **Sending original documents.** Always send copies.
- **Missing the deadline** in your state. A demand letter does not pause the statute of limitations.

## Should a lawyer review it?

For a property damage or diminished value claim, many people handle it themselves. For an injury claim, especially one with ongoing treatment, disputed fault or bills near the policy limit, having an attorney review the letter first is a smart move. Many offer free consultations.
