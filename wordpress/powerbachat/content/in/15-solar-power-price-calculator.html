What a unit of solar power really costs

The idea is simple. Take the total you pay for the system, after subsidy, and add the expected running costs, such as one inverter replacement and cleaning. Divide by the units the system will produce over its life. That gives a lifetime cost per unit.

For example, a 3 kW system produces roughly 350 units a month, or about 4,200 units a year. Over 25 years, allowing for a small decline in output each year, that is around 95,000 to 100,000 units. Divide your total cost by that number and the result is usually just a few rupees per unit, well below the upper slab rates in most states.

How the solar power price calculator works

  1. Size: your monthly units divided by what 1 kW produces in a month in India, rounded up to the nearest half kilowatt.
  2. Panels: the size divided by a typical modern panel's wattage.
  3. Cost: the size times the current installed price range per kW for DCR on-grid systems.
  4. Subsidy: the central PM Surya Ghar amount for that size.
  5. Saving: your estimated current bill from the slab tariff, of which solar offsets around 85 percent.
  6. Payback: your share of the cost divided by the yearly saving.

Things that change the price of solar power

  • Sunshine: sunnier states such as Rajasthan and Gujarat produce more units per kW, which lowers the cost per unit.
  • Shade and tilt: a shaded or flat installation produces less.
  • Dust: uncleaned panels in dusty cities lose output.
  • Self-use: units you use yourself save at your full tariff, while exported units are valued under your state's net metering rules.
  • Subsidy: the central subsidy lowers the cost per unit most for small systems.

A worked example

A family in Nagpur uses 400 units a month, much of it in Maharashtra's expensive upper slabs. The solar power price calculator suggests about 3.5 kW. After subsidy, their lifetime cost per solar unit comes to a few rupees, against a grid price that reaches well into double digits for their top units. Every unit of solar they use themselves replaces one of their most expensive grid units. The payback is short, and the saving continues for decades.

Solar power price versus grid price over time

Grid tariffs in India have generally risen over the years as the cost of power purchase and distribution grows. The cost of a solar unit, on the other hand, is mostly fixed on the day you install, because the sunshine is free. That makes solar a hedge against future tariff increases: if grid prices rise, your saving grows with them.

Self-use versus export

The value of a solar unit depends on what happens to it. A unit you use in your own home at midday saves a unit you would otherwise buy at your full grid rate, with duty on top. A unit you export goes to the grid and is credited under your state's net metering rules, which may value it differently. To get the lowest effective solar power price, shift flexible loads such as the washing machine, water pump, iron and EV charging into sunny hours. Families who do this get noticeably more from the same panels.

Degradation and long-term output

Solar panels lose a small fraction of their output each year, typically well under one percent for good panels. Over 25 years that adds up, which is why our lifetime estimate allows for a gentle decline. Performance warranties guarantee a minimum output at the end of the period, so check the figure in your panel warranty. Cleaning and a healthy inverter matter more for real output than degradation in the early years.

When solar is less attractive

If your use is very low, say under 100 units a month, or your state's tariff and subsidies keep your bill near zero, solar pays back slowly. Delhi homes under 200 units a month are a good example, because the state subsidy already covers their bill. In those cases, cutting waste and using efficient appliances may be the better first step.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cost of solar power per unit in India?

Over a system's life, usually a few rupees per unit after subsidy, depending on cost, sunshine and maintenance. That is well below upper grid slab rates in most states.

Does the solar power price calculator include maintenance?

The payback figure uses the installed cost. For a lifetime cost per unit, add an inverter replacement and cleaning, as explained above.

Is solar worth it if I use 200 units a month?

Often yes with the subsidy, especially in states with higher tariffs. Enter 200 in the calculator to see your payback.

Do exported units save as much as self-used units?

Not always. Self-used units save at your full tariff, while exported units are credited under your state's net metering terms.

How many units does 1 kW of solar produce in India?

Roughly 115 to 125 units a month on average, depending on location, tilt, shade and cleaning.

Will solar keep my bill at zero?

Rarely exactly zero, because fixed charges usually remain. A well-sized system can bring the energy part of the bill close to zero in many months.

Does the calculator account for monsoon months?

It uses yearly average sunshine. Monsoon months produce less and sunny months more, which roughly balance over a year.
