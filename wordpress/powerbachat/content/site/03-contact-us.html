Before you write

A few things will help us answer quickly.

  • If a calculation looks wrong, tell us your electricity company, the units on your bill, the billing month and the total you were charged. If you are in Pakistan, say whether your bill shows protected or non-protected.
  • If you want to report a rate change, a link to the notification or a photo of the relevant page is ideal.
  • If you are sending part of a bill, please hide your name, address, account or reference number and meter number first. We never need them.

What we can and cannot help with

If you have an electricity bill question, we can explain how a bill is calculated, why it might have changed, and what a solar or backup system might cost for your usage. We cannot change your bill, correct a meter reading, restore a connection or handle complaints about an electricity company. For those, contact your electricity company directly using the helpline or complaint number printed on your bill. If a complaint is not resolved, the regulator in your country has its own complaint process.

We also do not sell or install solar systems, and we are not linked to any installer. If you want quotes, our solar pages explain what to ask so you can compare them fairly.

Email

You can also write to us at [powerbachat_contact_email]. Please do not send passwords, card details or bank information by email or through the form; we will never ask for them.

Corrections and feedback

Corrections from readers have improved the site more than once. When a report leads to a change, we fix the data, test the affected calculators again and update the page. Our editorial policy explains how that works.

Your privacy

We use the details you send only to reply to you and, where relevant, to fix the site. We do not add you to any mailing list unless you join our tariff alerts separately. Our privacy policy has the details.
