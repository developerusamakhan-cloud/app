This UPPCL bill calculator is for domestic urban consumers in Uttar Pradesh, supplied by the state's distribution companies under the Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation: Madhyanchal, Purvanchal, Paschimanchal, Dakshinanchal and KESCo in Kanpur. Enter your monthly units above and you will see the slab charges, the fixed charge for a typical 2 kW connection, electricity duty and an estimated total.

Uttar Pradesh is a big state with very different households, from small flats in Lucknow to large family homes in Meerut and Varanasi. What they share is a single tariff set by the state commission, which makes a reliable estimate possible for anyone with an urban metered connection.

How to use the UPPCL bill calculator

  1. Take your previous reading from the last bill, the UPPCL app or your smart meter's display.
  2. Read the meter now and subtract. That is your units so far this month.
  3. Divide by the days so far and multiply by 30 to estimate the full month.
  4. Enter the result. The slip shows each slab, the fixed charge, duty and the total.

If you have a smart prepaid meter, the app shows daily units directly, which makes this even easier. Checking once a week is enough to spot a rising trend early. Families who do it regularly tell us the biggest benefit is not the saving itself but the end of surprises: when the bill or the recharge comes, the amount is roughly what they expected.

UPPCL rates used in the calculator

For urban domestic connections, the energy charge rises gently across four slabs. A fixed charge per kilowatt of sanctioned load is added every month, along with electricity duty.

Compared with many states, Uttar Pradesh's slabs climb slowly, so the average price per unit stays fairly steady as usage grows. That means every unit you save is worth roughly the same, whether you use 200 or 600 a month. It also means the fixed charge matters more for small users, because it is spread over fewer units.

Smart prepaid meters in Uttar Pradesh

UP has been replacing older meters with smart meters, many of them prepaid. With a prepaid meter you recharge in advance, and the balance falls as you use electricity. The tariff is the same, but the experience changes: you see your spending day by day and there is no surprise bill at the end of the month. If your balance runs low, supply can be cut until you recharge, so set a reminder. Use this calculator to estimate a month's spend, then plan your recharge around it.

Summer in the plains

From April to June, the plains of Uttar Pradesh are among the hottest places in India. Coolers run through the day and ACs through the night, and bills can triple. A few habits help a lot:

  • Use coolers in the dry heat of April, May and early June. They use a fraction of an AC's power.
  • Switch to the AC when humidity rises with the monsoon, and keep it at 25 or 26 degrees.
  • Shade the outdoor unit and keep its fins clean. Dust from the plains clogs them quickly.
  • Close curtains on west-facing rooms in the afternoon.

A worked example from Lucknow

A family in Gomti Nagar with a 2 kW connection averages 180 units in winter and 480 in June. Entering 480 in the UPPCL bill calculator shows the June bill, with most units in the top slab. They try 380 units, which they could reach by running one AC two hours less each night. The saving is modest per day but adds up over a long summer. Because UP's slabs are flat, they can predict the gain easily: each unit saved is worth roughly the top rate plus duty.

Power cuts and inverters

Supply in many UP cities has improved, but outages still happen, especially in summer evenings and in smaller towns. A home inverter with a tubular battery is common. Checking your bill and avoiding errors

Estimated or average readings are the most common source of wrong bills in Uttar Pradesh, especially where meter readers cannot access the meter. If your bill says the reading was assessed or average, photograph your meter with the date visible and compare. If the units are higher than your meter shows, raise a complaint through the UPPCL app, the 1912 helpline or your sub-division office before the due date. Keep the complaint number. Getting a reading corrected early is far easier than disputing a large catch-up bill later.

What the estimate leaves out

  • Arrears, late payment surcharge and meter rent where applicable.
  • Rural, BPL and commercial tariffs, which differ from urban domestic rates.

Is solar worth it in Uttar Pradesh?

The plains get strong sunshine for most of the year, and the central PM Surya Ghar subsidy covers a large part of a small system's cost. Frequently asked questions

Does the UPPCL bill calculator work for Noida and Ghaziabad?

Yes, for urban domestic connections supplied by the state discoms. Some areas are served by private licensees with their own billing, so check the name on your bill.

Is this the UPPCL bijli bill calculator for rural homes?

No. Rural domestic connections have different rates. This calculator uses the urban domestic tariff.

How is the fixed charge worked out?

It is charged per kilowatt of sanctioned load each month. Our estimate assumes 2 kW. Check your bill for your actual load.

How often are UPPCL rates updated?

The state commission reviews tariffs periodically. We last checked the rates in [powerbachat_checked].

Can I pay my UPPCL bill or recharge online?

Yes. Postpaid bills and prepaid recharges can be paid through the official UPPCL app and website, as well as common payment apps. Keep the receipt until the next bill shows the payment, so you have proof if it is missed.
