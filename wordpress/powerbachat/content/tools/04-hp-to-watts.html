One horsepower equals 745.7 watts, so to convert hp to watts you multiply the horsepower by 745.7. A 1.5 hp pool pump is about 1,119 watts and a 5 hp motor about 3,728 watts. Which horsepower?

There are three horsepower units in common use, and they differ by up to about 1.4 percent:

  • Mechanical or imperial horsepower: 745.7 watts. This is the usual hp in the United States and the United Kingdom, and the one to use unless you know otherwise.
  • Electrical horsepower: exactly 746 watts, used for rating electric motors.
  • Metric horsepower (PS, CV or pk): 735.5 watts, used for engines in much of Europe and Asia.

To get kilowatts, divide the watts by 1,000, or multiply horsepower by 0.7457. A 150 hp car engine is about 112 kW.

Motor horsepower is output, not input

The horsepower on a motor nameplate is the mechanical power at the shaft. The motor draws more electrical power than that, because some energy is lost as heat. A 1 hp motor that is 85 percent efficient draws about 745.7 ÷ 0.85 = 877 watts from the supply while working at full load.

Motors also draw a surge when they start, often several times their running current. When sizing a generator, inverter or solar system for a pump or compressor, allow for both the efficiency loss and the starting surge, or check the starting watts given by the manufacturer.

Worked examples

  • 1 hp water pump: 745.7 watts of output, around 850 to 950 watts drawn from the supply.
  • 2 hp air compressor: 2 × 745.7 = 1,491 watts at the shaft.
  • Half horsepower garage door opener: 0.5 × 745.7 = 373 watts.
  • 100 PS engine: 100 × 735.5 = 73,550 watts, or about 73.5 kW.

Frequently asked questions

How many watts is 1 hp?

One mechanical horsepower is 745.7 watts. An electrical horsepower is 746 watts and a metric horsepower is 735.5 watts.

How do I convert hp to kW?

Multiply the horsepower by 0.7457. For example, 10 hp × 0.7457 = 7.46 kW.

How many hp is 1,000 watts?

1,000 watts is about 1.34 mechanical horsepower, because 1,000 ÷ 745.7 = 1.341.

How much electricity does a 1 hp motor use per hour?

At full load, roughly 0.9 kWh per hour once motor losses are included. If it runs at part load the figure is lower, so a plug in energy meter gives the most accurate answer.
