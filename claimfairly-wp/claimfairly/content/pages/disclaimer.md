That sentence appears on every calculator. Here is what it means in full.

## Not legal advice

ClaimFairly provides general information and calculators for educational purposes. Nothing on this site is legal, financial, tax or medical advice, and using the site does not create an attorney-client relationship. {{site}} is not a law firm.

## Estimates, not guarantees

Our calculators use common formulas and the numbers you enter. Real claims depend on many things we cannot see: evidence, medical records, witness statements, insurance policy terms, your state's laws, the court and the people involved. Actual settlements can be higher or lower than any range shown here, or zero.

## State laws change

We review state information regularly and date every page, but laws change and exceptions exist. Always confirm deadlines and rules with official sources or a licensed attorney, especially before a deadline.

## No endorsement

Mentions of insurance companies or other businesses are for information only. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by or paid by any insurer named on this site.

## Your decisions

You are responsible for decisions you make about your claim. Before accepting an offer, signing a release or missing a deadline, consider speaking with a licensed attorney in your state. Many offer free consultations.
