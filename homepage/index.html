Our Story

Born From Love
& a Little Desperation

My husband George and I have always been the traveling, ocean-loving type — scuba diving, boating, exploring deserted beaches, or just spending time in our South Florida backyard. But every time George stepped outside, he'd get eaten alive.

On our destination scuba trips it was worse: extreme itching, allergic reactions, sleepless nights, and a cast-iron habit of forgetting bug spray. When he did remember it, he hated putting those chemicals all over him.

So I got to work. After months of testing essential oils and soothing botanicals, I finally nailed it — a formula that keeps bugs away without the chemicals, and a balm that calms bites when the spray gets left behind.
