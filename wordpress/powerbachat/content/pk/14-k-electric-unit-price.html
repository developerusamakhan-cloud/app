The K-Electric unit price is the question every Karachi household asks when the summer bill lands. The honest answer is that there is no single price. The rate depends on how many units you use, which slab each unit falls into, whether you are a protected consumer, and that month's fuel cost adjustment. This page sets out the current slab rates, what gets added to them, and how to work out what a unit really costs in your own home.

If you only need a total for your units, our K-Electric bill calculator does the maths in seconds. Keep reading if you want to understand the number.

The Karachi fuel cost adjustment

This is where Karachi differs from the rest of Pakistan. K-Electric runs its own power plants and buys power on its own terms, so its monthly fuel cost adjustment is worked out separately from the other companies. It is a per-unit amount, added or refunded, based on what fuel actually cost compared with what the tariff assumed. In some months it is close to the national figure, in others noticeably higher or lower.

Because it applies to every unit, the fuel adjustment can shift the real K-Electric unit price more than a change of slab does. When your bill arrives, find the FCA line and divide it by your units to see what it added per unit that month. Our fuel price adjustment guide explains the mechanics in more detail.

Taxes on every unit

On top of the slab rates and the fuel adjustment, two more charges raise the price of each unit:

  • GST at 18 percent on the energy charge and most adjustments.
  • Electricity duty, a small provincial percentage. Our estimates use 1.5 percent.

Larger bills can also carry income tax for consumers who are not on the active taxpayer list.

Peak and off-peak rates in Karachi

Connections with a higher sanctioned load, common in DHA, Clifton and other bungalow areas, usually have time-of-use meters. These record peak and off-peak units separately, and the peak K-Electric unit price is higher. Peak hours fall in the evening and vary by season, so check the times printed on your bill. Running laundry, the water pump and the iron outside peak hours cuts the average without reducing your comfort at all.

Unit price for shops and offices

Commercial connections in Karachi, such as shops, clinics and small offices, are billed on a commercial tariff with different rates from the household slabs above. If you run a small business from a residential meter, your units are charged as household units, but heavy business use can push the home into the top slabs very quickly. A separate commercial connection may be the cleaner option. Ask K-Electric about the requirements for your load.

Finding your own real unit price

The simplest way to know your actual K-Electric unit price is to work it out from your bill. Take the current charges, before arrears and surcharge, and divide by the units consumed. That number already includes every slab, tax and adjustment. Do it for a summer bill and a winter bill, and you will see clearly how much the slabs and fuel adjustment move your real cost.

That figure is also the right yardstick for decisions. If your real price per unit is high, a more efficient AC, LED lighting or a solar system pays back faster, because every unit saved is worth more to you.

Bringing your Karachi unit cost down

  • Stay out of the top slab. Trimming your last 50 units saves at your highest rate.
  • Use dry mode on humid nights. It removes moisture with less cooling, and the room feels comfortable at a higher temperature.
  • Check the water pump and geyser. A faulty float switch or an always-on geyser are common hidden loads in Karachi homes.
  • Consider rooftop solar. Karachi gets reliable sunshine for most of the year. See our solar panel price today and net metering vs net billing pages before talking to installers.

Frequently asked questions

What is the K-Electric unit price today?

The tables above show every slab rate, protected and non-protected, last checked in [powerbachat_checked]. The monthly fuel cost adjustment is added on top.

Is electricity more expensive in Karachi than in Lahore?

The base slab rates are the same. The difference, when there is one, comes from the monthly fuel cost adjustment, which is calculated separately for Karachi.

Why did my K-Electric unit price go up when my units did not?

Almost always because of the fuel cost adjustment or a new tariff notification. Compare the FCA line on this month's bill with last month's.

Where is the K-Electric rate list PDF?

Official tariff notifications are published as PDFs, but the slab tables on this page show the same household rates in a form that is easier to read on a phone, and they are updated whenever the tariff changes.
