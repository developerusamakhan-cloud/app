Everything on ClaimFairly, in one place. Start with a calculator if you want numbers for your own claim, or browse the guides if you want to understand how claims work first.

The page is grouped the way most people use the site:

- **Calculators** estimate a settlement range, diminished value, pain and suffering and your take-home amount, and write a demand letter. Each one shows its formula and runs in your browser.
- **Guides** explain each stage of a claim in plain English, from the first week after a crash to signing the release.
- **Settlement amounts** show what you would keep from a $25,000 to $250,000 settlement after fees, costs and liens.
- **Injuries and insurers** cover how common injuries are valued and how the largest insurers handle claims.
- **About the site** covers who runs ClaimFairly, how the calculators work, our editorial policy, privacy and disclaimer.
