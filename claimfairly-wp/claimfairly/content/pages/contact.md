The best way to reach me is by email: **[{{email}}](mailto:{{email}})**. I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

## Good reasons to write

- **You found an error.** A wrong deadline, a broken link, a formula that does not add up. Please include the page and, if you can, a source.
- **Something was confusing.** If a page did not make sense, others probably felt the same.
- **You want a new calculator** or a guide on a topic we have not covered.
- **Press and media questions** about diminished value, the 17c formula or how claims are valued.

## What I cannot help with

I am not a lawyer and cannot give advice about your specific claim, review your documents or tell you whether to accept an offer. For that, please contact a licensed attorney in your state. Your state bar association runs a lawyer referral service that can point you to one.

Please do not send medical records, claim documents or other sensitive personal information by email.
