Our Story

Born From Love (and a Little Desperation)

My husband George could never step outside in South Florida without getting eaten alive. Destination scuba trips were worse — itching, allergic reactions, sleepless nights, and bug spray he always managed to forget. When he did remember it, he hated the chemicals.

So I got to work. After months of testing essential oils and soothing botanicals, I created a spray that actually keeps bugs away and a balm that calms bites when the spray gets left behind. What started as a home remedy for George became No No Mosquito.
